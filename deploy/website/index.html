Built for people with many mailboxes

Unified inbox

Every mailbox in one list, clearly labelled — or open one mailbox at a time. Conversations are grouped automatically.

Always the right sender

Replies go out from the mailbox the message came to. Undo send, scheduled sending, signatures and templates.

Private notifications

"New email received at sales@yourcompany.com" — never the sender or subject on your lock screen.

Phishing warnings

Fake senders, look-alike domains and deceptive links are flagged before you click. Block senders in one tap.

Works with your providers

Any mailbox with IMAP and SMTP: cPanel, Hostinger, Titan, Zoho, Fastmail and more. Settings are found automatically.

Your data stays yours

Mailbox passwords and files are encrypted. Your mail stays on your provider; disconnect any time and choose what we keep.

How it works

  1. Create your account with your Gmail address (it is only used to sign in and for account notices — your mailboxes stay separate).
  2. Get approved. We confirm your account and activate your access period.
  3. Connect your mailboxes with their email address and password. That's it — your mail arrives in AllWebMails, even while the app is closed.
